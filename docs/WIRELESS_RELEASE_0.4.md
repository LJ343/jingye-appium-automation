# Wireless recovery 0.4 — 2026-11-09

0.3 could attempt to enable wireless debugging, observe an immediate system reset, and then wait indefinitely for an external enable. 0.4 treats a setting that fails to remain enabled as a soft recovery failure: while Wi-Fi is available, it schedules two additional attempts after 60 and 120 seconds, with a fresh four-second Wi-Fi stability check before each write. One episode has at most three writes, and the existing persisted maximum of three writes per ten minutes still applies. Deadlines and consumed retry budgets are committed before setting writes and survive service recreation. Duplicate callbacks and Wi-Fi churn do not replenish that episode. An externally enabled switch must remain stable for 30 seconds before the episode is rearmed.

After three unsuccessful writes, RETRY_EXHAUSTED is explicit and further automatic writes stop. Missing permissions, rejected writes, platform/storage faults and the global rate limit remain protective stops. This is bounded recovery, not an unlimited retry loop. Android network-trust approval remains a user decision. Force-stop, reboot and an OS that kills the helper permanently are not covered by this acceptance.

The switch watcher remains event-driven. AddressReporter, AddressProtocol, ReporterSettings, StatusProvider and FailureBoundary are byte-identical to 0.3. Authenticated reporting stays at most once per 15 seconds while armed, Wi-Fi is available and the switch is on. No reduction of that interval is claimed or introduced. The PC uses a healthy existing connection; discovery and reconnect are on demand when connection checks fail. Status viewing stays read-only. Pairing trust must already be valid; connection recovery does not perform new pairing.

Same-package, same-certificate 0.4 was installed and its actual base.apk SHA256 verified. Original grants and private reporter configuration were preserved. The helper is armed with its foreground service; an authenticated report completed its challenge and the temporary receiver closed. The PC status returned CONNECTED with same-phone identity. In this healthy path the cumulative setting-write count stayed unchanged. ADB, Appium and OpenClaw instances, desktop source/configuration, business rules and the USB main branch were unchanged. The initial installer client timed out while Honor confirmation remained pending; the original timeout was retained, confirmation completed, and the installed artifact was checked without resubmitting installation.

Offline checks exercise the exact morning immediate-reset sequence, retry deadlines, persisted budget/deadline handling across recreation, callback storms, Wi-Fi loss, hard protective stops and actual StateStore commit failures. See HELPER_RETRY_VALIDATION.json and tests/run_offline_export.py. The public runner selects the current 41 policy and 9 storage tests explicitly; the retained ExternalWaitReviewTest is a historical 0.3 fixture and is not a current 0.4 test. The storage stubs are offline fixtures outside src and are not packaged in the APK.

No real switch-off/reset fault was injected and no new business task was run in this update. Natural IP change, arbitrary roaming/network recovery, force-stop recovery, reboot and a new long business run are not claimed. Original PC discovery port range 37000–50000 and 300-second budget remain. Public source contains no real provisioning data, account data, logs, signing private key, SDK dependencies or business attachments. Older release docs/manifests describe their dated revisions.
